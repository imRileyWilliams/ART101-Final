About Our Game

After a few failed relationships, you're finally ready to give love another chance! Encouraged by your friend, Ilia, you decide to attend a college blind date mixer, where you'll meet three interesting individuals: HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Get to know each character through individual conversations, make dialogue choices, and see how your responses affect their affection toward you. Your choices will shape each relationship and determine how your story unfolds.

At the end of each character's route, you'll decide whether to pursue romance or remain friends. Will you find love, make new friends, or maybe experience a little bit of both?
