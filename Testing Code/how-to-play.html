How to Play

Now that you've been dragged out to this college blind date mixer, it's time to get to know three possible suitors! The sweet HTML, the sassy CSS, and the kind JavaScript.

Approach each cute candidate and talk for a while. You'll be given two options for how to respond to what they say, your choices will affect how they feel about you.

You can see whether you're hitting it off or better as friends with the Affection Meter underneath the diolouge box. After getting to know your potential partners, you'll be matched with the one you vibe with the most, based on final affection points.
